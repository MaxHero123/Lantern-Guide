Lantern GuideLights the way for you

A friendly companion that helps older adults stay safe, organized and connected.

Lantern Guide catches scam messages with its own trained model, reads small print out loud, keeps a simple planner with medicine reminders, and finds local events and volunteering that fit each person's interests, in any town in the US.

Why I built it

It started with my grandparents.

Older adults get more scam texts and emails than anyone, and the scams keep getting more convincing. Small print on medicine labels gets harder to read. And after retiring, it can be hard to find things to do nearby, and people to do them with.

I wanted one calm, simple app that helps with all three, designed from the start for people who aren't comfortable with technology.

Made to be easy

Big buttons, large high-contrast text, plain words, and every answer explained. Nothing to sign up for, and nothing personal ever leaves the computer it runs on.

What it does

Four things, done simply

Is this message a scam?

  • Paste a text or email, or use a screenshot.
  • A scam model I trained myself, plus warning-sign rules, gives a clear answer: scam, junk, not sure, or fine.
  • It explains why in plain words, such as a fake website pretending to be the Postal Service.
  • One tap to call a family member before doing anything.

A planner that knows your day

  • Medicines, meals, appointments and plans in one place.
  • Gentle reminders the day before and right before something starts.
  • It notices conflicts, such as a medicine time during an outing, and suggests what to do.

Things to do and ways to help, nearby

  • Local events from official town, library and county calendars, plus local news organizations.
  • Volunteering grouped into kinds of help that suit older adults: food and meals, children and learning, animals, parks and museums, from home, and helping neighbors.
  • Jobs, internships and student-only roles are filtered out. Results refresh by themselves.

Works on a phone, in the colors you like

  • Three text sizes and four color schemes, all easy to read.
  • Read and magnify: take a photo of a recipe or label to see it big and hear it read aloud.
  • Weather and what to wear, on the home screen.

How it works

Under the hood

My own scam model

An ensemble I trained: a classic text model (70%) combined with a RoBERTa language model, plus rules that spot copycat websites, gift-card requests and urgent threats.

Private by design

Messages are checked on the computer itself, and never sent to an AI company. Answers like medicines are saved only on that computer.

Only verified sources

News comes only from real news organizations, events from official calendars, and places from OpenStreetMap. Every item names where it came from.

Any town

Type any US town and it finds the nearby towns, county, libraries and official calendars on its own, with no setup and no paid services.

Smart matching

Interests are searched with related words: "movies" also finds film, cinema and Hollywood. Results are scored for how well they suit older adults.

Built with

Python, scikit-learn, PyTorch and Transformers, and plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Free data from Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap, Wikidata, Idealist and more.

Try it

Run it on your computer

Lantern Guide runs on Mac, Windows or Linux with Python 3.10 or newer. In a terminal, inside the project folder:

python3 -m venv .venv
source .venv/bin/activate        # Windows: .venv\Scripts\activate
pip install -r requirements.txt
python app.py

Then open http://localhost:8000. Full instructions are in the README.
