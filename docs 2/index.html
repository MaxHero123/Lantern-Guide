Scam protection

Not sure about a message? Ask Lantern first.

Paste a suspicious text or email, or use a screenshot. Lantern Guide tells you in plain words whether it looks like a scam, junk or a normal message, and what to do next.

  • Checks texts, emails and screenshots
  • Explains why in everyday words, never just a score
  • Spots fake look-alike websites, like a link pretending to be the Postal Service
  • Catches gift-card requests, urgent threats and "Grandma, it's me" family scams
  • When it isn't sure, it says so and suggests checking with family
  • One button to call a family member before you act
  • Powered by a scam-detection model trained by Max. See how it works

Everything in one place

One trusted helper instead of a dozen apps.

Twelve things Lantern Guide does today, each set up around your own routine, town and interests.

Scam checker

Paste a text or email, or add a screenshot, and get a clear answer in seconds.

  • Four answers: scam, junk, not sure, or looks normal
  • Reasons in plain words, plus what to do next
  • Spots look-alike links, like "paypa1.com" posing as PayPal

Medicine reminders

Add each medicine once, with the times you take it.

  • A pop-up reminder when it's time, with a "Done" button
  • Heads-up when a dose falls during an outing: bring it, or take it early if your doctor allows
  • Meals are optional, and never nag

Planner

Your week at a glance, and the whole month when you need it.

  • Add doctor's appointments months ahead
  • Reminders the day before and again before it starts
  • The home screen shows the next 6 hours

Read and magnify

Take a photo of a label, letter or recipe to see the words big.

  • Press "Read it to me" to hear it out loud
  • A live magnifier that uses the camera like a magnifying glass
  • Photos are read on the device and never sent to a company

Events nearby

Things to do in and around your town, matched to your interests.

Ways to help that suit older adults, sorted into six kinds of help.

News and your teams

Local stories from real news organizations, and the teams you follow.

Privacy and security

Your life stays yours.

Your messages are checked by Lantern Guide's own scam model, not sent to an AI company. Your answers, like medicines and plans, are private to you.
